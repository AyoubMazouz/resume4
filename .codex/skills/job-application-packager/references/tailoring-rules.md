# Tailoring Rules

## Objective

Tailor resume content to the selected post while preserving factual accuracy.

## Allowed Changes

- Reorder skills to match role requirements.
- Rewrite bullet emphasis to highlight relevant accomplishments.
- Adjust resume headline and summary phrasing.
- Align EN and FR versions to the same factual claims.

## Forbidden Changes

- Do not invent experience, tools, certifications, dates, or responsibilities.
- Do not increase years/seniority beyond evidence.
- Do not claim ownership or impact not supported by existing resume content.

## Tailoring Sequence

1. Extract job requirement keywords.
2. Map each keyword to existing evidence in resume sources.
3. Prioritize matching skills in `technicalSkills`.
4. Rephrase experience bullets to foreground role-relevant outcomes.
5. Keep chronology and company history unchanged.
6. Mirror equivalent edits in both EN and FR content.

## Fit Label Guidance

- `Best fit`: strong stack + level alignment.
- `Good fit`: moderate alignment with one manageable gap.
- `Stretch`: significant level or stack gap.

Include one risk in post-details output even for best-fit cases.
