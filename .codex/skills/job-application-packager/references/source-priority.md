# Source Priority and Confidence

Use this source order for company and personnel research.

## Priority Order

1. Official company website and legal pages
2. Official LinkedIn company page
3. Official social accounts linked by company
4. Reputable business directories or press
5. Employee and recruiter LinkedIn profiles

## Confidence Labels

- `high`: direct first-party source or clearly attributable official profile.
- `medium`: likely accurate but not first-party or partially outdated.
- `low`: weak attribution, indirect source, or conflicting signals.

## Recording Rules

- Add source links for each non-trivial claim.
- If a claim cannot be verified, write `Not verified`.
- If data is unavailable, write `Not found`.
- Prefer fewer high-confidence facts over many weak claims.
