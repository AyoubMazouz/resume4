# Fit Scoring

Apply this rubric to each posting against the candidate profile.

## Best fit

Assign when most are true:
- Role family matches primary target.
- Seniority aligns or is at most one step above profile.
- Core stack overlap is strong (about 70 percent or more).
- Location/work mode is compatible.
- No hard blocker is visible.

## Good fit

Assign when:
- Role family matches primary or secondary target.
- Core stack overlap is moderate (about 50-70 percent).
- One manageable gap exists.

## Stretch

Assign when any apply:
- Seniority is clearly above profile.
- Multiple must-have requirements are missing.
- Hard constraints conflict (location, language, contract model, mandatory domain).

## Required fields per scored job

- `Fit label`: Best fit / Good fit / Stretch
- `Why`: two short reasons tied to profile evidence
- `Risk`: one concrete gap or blocker
